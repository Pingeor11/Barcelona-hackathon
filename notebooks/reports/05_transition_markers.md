# 05 — Transition markers

- A transition marker identifies a change in plausible team control, not merely a nearest-player change.
- The marker combines previous support, ball state, candidate receiver, opponent arrival margin, path agreement, and later controlled evidence.
- During a reception race, both teams may be close; the marker remains unresolved until the evidence separates.
- The first H/A observation and the later comfortable anchor are tracked separately so timing bias is visible.
- A direction change can indicate a first-time pass or a deflection; team targeting and subsequent control decide how it is interpreted.
- The transition timeline is compared with the public H/A/D bar without using that bar to make the decision.
