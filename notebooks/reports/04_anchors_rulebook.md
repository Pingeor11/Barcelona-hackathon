# 04 — Anchors and rule book

- An anchor is a frame where the evidence is strong enough to support H/A or D.
- **Control anchor:** ball/player path intersection, compatible speed change, team margin, low pressure, and persistence.
- **Restart anchor:** Smart Tagging context plus restart geometry and the first credible controlled receiver.
- **Dead-ball anchor:** near-zero ball activity and boundary/restart evidence; preserve D while play is unresolved.
- **Flight/contest:** do not force H/A from proximity alone; retain D or a provisional transition state.
- **Long-ball reception:** high travel distance and density require landing, deceleration, path agreement, and receiver persistence before anchoring.
- Anchor plots show the football pitch, coordinate paths, player support, speed evidence, scenario timeline, and GT bar when available.
- No fixed-duration D hold is required; the anchor is promoted by converging evidence.
